## Comparability

- Merged and unmerged (per-CE) tasks differ: merged `nist20_inchikey` is 100% FT `[M+H]+` HCD, 1.01 rows per training molecule; unmerged `nist20_ma_mi_inchikey` has 18.55 rows per molecule, so 0.80 merged against 0.69 unmerged "was never like for like" ([source](../findings/masster/masster_memorization_audits.md#merged-vs-unmerged-task-exposure-and-merged-baseline-from-local-memory-verified-2026-10-06)).
- Merge target changed around 2026-09-03 (`spec_params.merge_method` absent to `tolerance_consensus`): 105 old-merge runs never beat 0.7515, the new cohort reaches 0.78-0.80; check `config.spec_params.merge_method` before comparing across that date (same source).
- The i-line (`i1` onward) is on the relabelled `ORB`/`ORB_F` NIST20 (`data/split/nist20_inchikey`, 157,678 / 52,020 / 52,621 spectra) and is the control for new arms; `FT`-label results (`c0`, `f0`, `f1`, `n4` and their arms) are retired and are not tabulated here. Deltas measured on `FT` labels "are not guaranteed to carry over" ([source](../findings/datasets/nist_orbitrap_instrument_ce_scales_2026_09_28.md#8-training-with-the-instrument-input-i1-and-i2-running-read-2026-09-28)).
- Metrics: logged `cos_sim_0.01` uses hard 0.01 Da bins; `cos_hun_inst_tol` uses 10 ppm with a 200 Da floor (FT) and one-to-one assignment. On `i1`@59 val they read 0.7622 and 0.7880 ([source](../findings/masster/masster_i1_worst_predictions_2026_09_30.md#method)). CPU and CUDA build different float32 bin grids, so a CPU rescoring of `i1` val reads 0.7611 against the logged 0.7622 ([source](../findings/masster/masster_i1_residual_structure_2026_09_28.md#side-finding-cos_sim_001-depends-on-the-device)).
- 0.1 Da binning (GLACIER) against 10 ppm: a 0.1 Da window is +0.029 overall for `pqx7ywc9` but +0.008 on FT `[M+H]+` and +0.38 on QTOF; 0.01 Da hard bins score 0.045 below +-0.005 Da tolerance matching ([source](../notes/glacier_object_detection.md#controlled-tolerance-sweep--the-number-that-actually-answers-is-01-da-easier-than-10-ppm)).
- Tail means (epochs 54-63 on the 64-epoch i-line, 118-127 on the 128-epoch merged screen) and single-epoch or best-so-far values differ: the audit records `d1`/`d2`/`d3` as +0.0053/+0.0052/+0.0084 on the tail while earlier documents quote best-so-far +0.0043/+0.0041/+0.0072 ([source](../findings/masster/masster_memorization_audits.md#merged-pre-plan-attempts-control-c4a_base-mean)).
- Gates: i-line seed spread 0.0042, single-seed gate +0.005; merged 128-epoch screen gate +-0.005 against a replicate spread of 0.0007; one-hour merged slices resolve about +-0.007; the local 3,000/1,000-molecule screen has a control-pair tail spread of 0.0076. A single unmerged arm delta has a standard error of about 0.003 ([source](../findings/masster/masster_memorization_audits.md#noise-floor-and-the-gate)).
- Selected worst sets are biased toward the selecting checkpoint: `i1`@63 is a same-run neighbour of the selector `i1`@59 (per-spectrum correlation 0.900 against 0.861-0.867 between separate runs), so floor and tail increments need an independent selector ([source](../findings/masster/masster_i1_worst_predictions_2026_09_30.md#result-fa1-on-the-floor-follow-up-2026-09-30)).
- Splits differ by model: the i-line uses `nist20_inchikey` (60/20/20 by 2D InChIKey); FraGNNet and the unmerged `ma_mi` runs use `nist20_ma_mi_inchikey`; the two are independent draws ([source](../findings/masster/masster_smarts_findings.md#g0-m-h--templates-on)).
- GLACIER's 0.838 is on a random 80/10/10 split at 0.1 Da with per-CE spectra; our split is 60/20/20; the FraGNNet row in GLACIER's table is adapted from Wang et al. (ICEBERG 2.0), not produced under our evaluation ([source](../notes/glacier_object_detection.md#against-their-table-15)).
